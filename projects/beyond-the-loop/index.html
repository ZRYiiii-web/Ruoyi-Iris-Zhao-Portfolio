01 / BEYOND THE LOOP

When attention shapes experience

Personalized feeds can make the mechanism of selection hard to see. Research and case studies explore how an immersive experience can help people notice the relationship between preference, exposure and information diversity.

02 / BEYOND THE LOOP

Making an algorithm spatial

A commercial street, library, maze and balance space translate abstract ideas into places to explore. Gaze and choice form part of a feedback loop, while exploration beyond familiar preferences introduces opportunities to experience change.

03 / BEYOND THE LOOP

Experiencing the loop

The final scenes turn recommendation logic into a visible environment. The work asks how interaction can invite reflection on the systems shaping everyday information, and how agency can be made perceptible within that experience.
