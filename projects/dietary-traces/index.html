01 / DIETARY TRACES

A distant consequence

The effects of everyday eating habits unfold over time, making them difficult to perceive in the moment. The research considers how health information can become a more accessible, embodied learning experience.

02 / DIETARY TRACES

Turning information into interaction

Food cards and a quantity dial make input physical. Users explore changes across brain, cardiovascular, musculoskeletal and liver-related views. The design connects selecting, adjusting, observing and reflecting within one interaction loop.

03 / DIETARY TRACES

Building the installation

The prototype combines an NFC card interface, a physical control panel and a screen. Interface states, organ views and a printed receipt give different forms to the same underlying educational narrative.

04 / DIETARY TRACES

An experience to take away

Model making, hardware coding and on-site testing inform the final installation. The project uses an illustrative model to support learning about long-term habits, with the receipt inviting visitors to reflect after the interaction.
