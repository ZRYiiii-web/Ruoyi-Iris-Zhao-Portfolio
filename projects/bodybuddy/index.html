01 / EDUCATION · PLAY · SERVICE DESIGN

BodyBuddy

A gentler way to learn about the body.

A body-education toolkit for girls in underserved rural communities. Projection, cards and guided play support learning, alongside a service concept connecting teachers and donors.

ROLE
Product & Interaction Designer
TIMELINE
2025.3–2025.9
METHODS

01 / BODYBUDDY

Learning with care

The project examines access to body knowledge, age-appropriate learning and the role of teachers. Interviews, research and stakeholder mapping establish a context for approachable educational experiences.

02 / BODYBUDDY

Knowledge through guided play

Projection and cards create a shared focal point for learning. Card categories cover understanding, protection, help-seeking and emotional support, with a physical scoring element supporting the game structure.

03 / BODYBUDDY

Beyond the object

The prototype is situated within classroom use and a wider support service. The companion app concept includes teaching guidance, donation tracking and teaching uploads, connecting the object with the people who make it useful.
